# Privacy policy — JustMyFeed

Last updated: 13 November 2026

## The short version

JustMyFeed collects nothing, sends nothing, and talks to no server. Everything it does happens
inside your own browser.

## What the extension stores

Two settings, kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your own computer:

- whether the extension is switched on or off;
- the cover image you chose, if you chose one, stored as the image itself.

That is all. This data never leaves your computer. It is not synced, not backed up by us, and not
readable by us. Removing the extension removes it.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.
- It does not track browsing history, clicks, or which posts were covered.
- It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or error reporting.
- It makes no network requests of any kind.
- It does not read or store the content of the pages you visit. It inspects the page only in your
  browser's memory, to decide what to cover, and keeps no record of it.
- It does not read or touch your accounts, passwords or messages.

## Permissions, and why each one is needed

- **Storage** — to remember the two settings above.
- **Access to youtube.com, instagram.com and facebook.com** — the extension has to run on those
  pages to find Reels and Shorts and cover them. It runs nowhere else.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's source repository.
